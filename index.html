Jiahao Ji 季嘉豪

I am a full-time Algorithm Researcher at Noitom Robotics. My work focuses on embodied motion data, scalable task generation, Real2Sim2Real pipelines, and 3D reconstruction for robotics.

I am interested in building structured data and learning systems that connect human motion, physical interaction, and robot intelligence. Before joining Noitom, I worked with Seed Robotics at ByteDance on digital asset reconstruction and dexterous manipulation.
